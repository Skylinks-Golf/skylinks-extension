            • PaceAverage units sold per week over the last 4 weeks, from Lightspeed.
            • ParPace × weeks until the next delivery, plus a cushion.
            • CasesWhat's needed to get back to par, rounded up to whole cases (case sizes from Lightspeed). Never more than about 4 weeks of sales, but 1 case is always OK.
